# Posted Up (pathway P15, draft SRD v3.10.30)

> The Vein comes up through the city in three places: the Corner, the Tunnel and the Lot. Every round, before casting, the crews choose where to post up. A d6 says where the Vein runs rich. Then everyone casts as normal, knowing who is standing where.

Source: `Docs/Concept Rework/10_Posted_Up.md` and `HOOD_SORCERY_SRD_v3.10.30_DRAFT.md`. **Everything in Classic stays**; three steps are added.

## The round (new steps in bold)

1. **Spin.** **The Vein runs rich at one Spot** (d6: 1–2 Corner, 3–4 Tunnel, 5–6 Lot).
2. **Post up.** Everyone picks a Spot at once (the finger throw: 1 Corner, 2 Tunnel, 3 Lot).
3. **Cast** as in Classic. You can see who shares your Spot.
4. **Flip** and **rank** exactly as in Classic: all crews, same matrix, same Wheel effects.
5. **Take the Spots.** At each Spot, the crew with the **highest ATK total** there takes it (normal ties). A crew alone takes its Spot for free. A Spot nobody stood on keeps its holder.
6. **The holder of the rich Spot heals 1** (not below 0).
7. Refill.

**The Vein closes.** After the last round, **each Spot you hold is −1 damage**. Then least damage wins (Classic ties).

**Rulings.** The Spot contest uses ATK **totals** (after Thumbs and Flip the Script), not ranks, so Mugged and Sabotage don't change who takes a Spot. A crew can hold several Spots.

## Modifiers that fit

- **Forecast:** from round 4 the Vein announces next round's rich Spot.
- **Call Out (P16):** spend a Thumb to call out a crew **at your Spot**.
